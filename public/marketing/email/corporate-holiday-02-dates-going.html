Corporate dinners

What is actually still open in December.

I wrote to you a couple of weeks ago about bringing a kitchen into your own space rather than renting a ballroom. This is the short follow-up, and it is only useful if it is honest, so:

Fridays in the second and third weeks are the first to go every year, and they are going. Weeknights in the first two weeks of December are wide open and are genuinely the better evening for a company dinner — people are not yet exhausted, and nobody is racing to another party.

 

If you have thirty to sixty people — plated dinner in your own space works cleanly at this size. Three courses, two choices on the main, everyone with a dietary requirement handled without a separate conversation on the night.

If you have sixty to a hundred and fifty — stations. A raw and cold station, a seafood station, something carved in front of people. It keeps the room moving and talking rather than seated in rows.

If you have under thirty — this is where it gets interesting. At that size I can cook something I could not attempt for a hundred, and the evening feels like a dinner party rather than an event.

Carved at the table. The part people photograph.

Tell me the date and the number. If I am free I will send a menu; if I am not, I will say so straight away rather than leaving you waiting.
