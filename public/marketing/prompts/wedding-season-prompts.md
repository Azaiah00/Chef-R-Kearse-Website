# Image prompts — Wedding Season

For Nano Banana. Six prompts, each one naming exactly where the output goes.

---

## The rule these are all written under

**No generated image may ever depict a dish a guest could order**, and for weddings there is a second rule on top of it:

**No generated image may depict a wedding that did not happen.** No couples, no first dances, no guests raising glasses, no bride at a table. A generated wedding photograph implies he catered that wedding. He did not, because it does not exist, and a couple who later works that out has every right to be furious.

So what is left, and what these prompts cover: the room before anyone is in it, the light, the table, the texture, the surface. Empty spaces and close details. That is genuinely enough — an empty barn at golden hour is a more evocative wedding image than a stock couple, and it has the advantage of being honest.

Where a campaign needs to show food, it uses his own photographs, listed by filename in `wedding-season-ads.md`.

**Every generated file gets the suffix `-gen`** so nobody ever has to guess which images were photographed.

## 01 — The barn, dressed, empty, golden hour

**Where it goes:** the wedding page hero alternative, email header, Concept C background. Landscape 3:2 and a 9:16 crop.

```
The interior of a restored wooden barn dressed for a wedding dinner, completely
empty of people, photographed in the last twenty minutes of daylight. Two long
trestle tables end to end with white linen, simple greenery down the centre,
mismatched wooden chairs, low candles not yet lit. Enormous open doorway at the
far end letting in low golden sun that rakes across the floorboards and up the
table. Exposed beams, weathered timber, string lights hanging unlit. Shot at 28mm,
f/2.8, from the dark end looking toward the light. Backlit, warm, dust in the air.
Honeyed amber and deep timber brown. Fine film grain. No people at all. No food on
the tables. No signage, no logos, no readable text. Photorealistic, editorial
wedding photography.
```

**Negative:** people, couples, guests, food, plated dishes, cool light, flash, confetti, balloons, text, logos, anything that implies an event in progress.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/wedding-barn-golden-gen.webp`

## 02 — Dark surface for the testimonial type

**Where it goes:** Concept B in `wedding-season-ads.md`, where the review quote is set over it. 4:5 and 9:16.

```
A very dark, quiet surface for text to sit on. Deep charcoal with a warm brown
undertone, the faintest suggestion of linen weave, a soft warm pool of candlelight
entering low from the bottom left and falling to near-black at the top. Almost no
detail. Nothing competing for attention. Fine film grain. Charcoal, near-black,
one warm amber gradient. No objects, no food, no people, no text, no pattern. 
Photorealistic texture, not illustration.
```

**Negative:** objects, food, people, text, busy pattern, cool tones, anything purple or blue, hard edges.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/surface-wedding-dark-gen.webp`

## 03 — Two place settings, close, nothing served

**Where it goes:** email section breaks, the tasting invitation header, carousel backgrounds. Square 1:1.

```
Two place settings side by side on white linen, photographed close and at a low
angle. Empty white plates, polished cutlery, two wine glasses, a folded napkin
with a sprig of greenery laid on it, a low candle between them just lit. Warm
candlelight as the only source, roughly 2400K, falling away fast into shadow. Shot
at 85mm, f/2, focus on the near napkin and greenery, the second setting softening.
Very shallow depth of field. Cream, warm amber, deep shadow. Fine grain. The
plates must be completely empty. No food, no hands, no faces, no text, no logos.
Photorealistic, editorial still life.
```

**Negative:** food on plates, any dish, hands, faces, people, cool light, flash, text, logos, plastic.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/wedding-place-setting-gen.webp`

## 04 — The empty dressed table at dusk, outdoors

**Where it goes:** Concept C in the ad brief, plus story frames. Vertical 9:16.

```
A long outdoor dining table dressed for a wedding at dusk, entirely empty of
people. White linen, simple greenery, glassware, candles in glass hurricanes
lit against the failing light. Set on grass beneath mature trees, string lights
overhead just beginning to register. Deep blue-violet sky at the top of frame
giving way to warm candle and bulb light at table level. Shot at 35mm, f/1.8,
from the end of the table looking along it, the far end dissolving into dark.
Warm amber pools against cool dusk. Fine grain, slight haze. No people. No food on
the table. No text, no logos, no signage. Photorealistic, editorial.
```

**Negative:** people, couples, guests, food, plated dishes, harsh flash, daylight, text, logos.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/wedding-table-dusk-gen.webp`

## 05 — Linen, greenery and wax, macro

**Where it goes:** texture behind type, email dividers, the quiet frames in a carousel. Square 1:1.

```
Extreme close-up of a wedding table surface. Heavy white linen with a visible
weave, a single eucalyptus sprig laid across it, a pool of set candle wax at the
base of a glass holder, the foot of a wine glass throwing a small bright caustic
onto the cloth. One warm low light source, everything else in deep shadow. Macro,
100mm, f/2.8, focus on the eucalyptus leaf, background dissolving completely. Very
shallow. Cream, sage green, warm amber, deep brown. Fine film grain. No food, no
hands, no faces, no text, no logos. Photorealistic, editorial still life.
```

**Negative:** food, hands, faces, text, logos, cool light, hard flash, bright even lighting.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/texture-wedding-linen-gen.webp`

## 06 — The venue kitchen, empty and ready

**Where it goes:** the venue coordinator email, and the "he walks your kitchen" message. Landscape 3:2.

```
A small commercial kitchen in a wedding venue, empty and clean before service.
Stainless steel worktops, a six-burner range, hanging pans, an empty pass with a
heat lamp switched off, one high window letting in late afternoon light. Clean,
used, characterful rather than showroom-new. Warm daylight from the window
crossing the steel, deep shadows in the corners, one overhead light on. Shot at
24mm, f/4, from the doorway. Documentary, warm despite being steel, calm before
the work starts. Amber daylight against cool steel, charcoal shadow. Fine grain.
No people, no food anywhere in frame. No text, no logos, no brand names on
equipment. Photorealistic.
```

**Negative:** food, ingredients, people, chefs, cool clinical lighting, brand names on equipment, text, logos, mess.

**Save as:** `public/images/marketing/venue-kitchen-gen.webp`

## After generating

1. Save at 1600px on the long edge, WebP, quality 80.
2. Put them in `public/images/marketing/`, kept separate from `public/images/dishes/` so photographed work and generated atmosphere never get confused.
3. Check every one against both rules at the top. Any plate with anything on it, or any hint of a couple or a guest, means regenerate.
4. Alt text describes the room, the light or the texture. It never implies a dish, and it never implies an event that happened.

---

## What to shoot rather than generate

Honestly, the strongest wedding imagery he could have is not on this list — it is a real wedding he actually caters, photographed properly. One good set from one real event beats every prompt here.

So: at the next wedding he books, get the couple's permission in writing in advance, and bring a photographer for two hours. The shot list is in `ASSET-BRIEF.md`. After that, these prompts become the filler they should be, and his own work carries the campaign.
